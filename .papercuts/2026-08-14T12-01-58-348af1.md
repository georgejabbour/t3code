Tracing which worktree the archived-thread reaper removed was impossible from the logs. ArchivedThreadReaper.removeWorktree spans carry only the server startup attributes (server.mode, server.port, startup.phase) and no threadId or worktreePath, so ten removal spans could not be matched to any folder. Fix: annotate that span with threadId and worktreePath, the way GitVcsDriver.removeWorktree annotates git.cwd.
